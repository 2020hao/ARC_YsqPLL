# Source attribution

- HYDRA kernels retain the embedded credit to Angus Dempster, Daniel F. Schmidt
  and Geoffrey I. Webb, *HYDRA: Competing Convolutional Kernels for Fast and
  Accurate Time Series Classification*, https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.13652 .
- The arc-study HYDRA adaptation and its original source metadata came from
  Ferracuti et al.'s release, https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15279701 . Source
  declarations are preserved. The current local classifier/parameters are not
  represented as an exact reproduction of their best full pipeline.
- `third_party/current_reference_recovery_v2` preserves the user-provided RCR
  research reference. CFT is the separately named local candidate, not an
  improvement attributed to the reference author.
- ArcFENet is a local reconstruction based on
  https://doi.org/10.1109/TIM.2026.3704190 . ArcFusionNet-current is a local
  current-only adaptation based on https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aei.2026.104680 .
  These are not author-released pretrained weights.

No new blanket open-source license is assigned by this refactor. Public hosting
and source attribution do not replace the applicable third-party terms. Dataset
licenses and acknowledgments remain attached to the original data releases;
acquisition files and author MATLAB datasets are not distributed here.
